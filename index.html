Welcome

True Grace Ministries is an interdenominational Christian ministry and one resource among many for disciples who seek ever more intimate communion with Christ within the household of faith, and who seek to engage the world in loving witness as ambassadors of God's Kingdom (Heb. 3:1-6; John 17:20-26; Col. 1:23,13).


"Great minds talk about ideas, average minds talk about things, small minds talk about other people."


Our Mission

The mission of True Grace Ministries encompasses both an internet as well as local community ministry and includes, but is not limited to:


  • Community & Care Ministry: Enlisting and engaging ministers and chaplains (both ordained and lay members) to minister in their community especially to seniors and those with physical and other impairments who may not be able to attend services regularly at a local church. This includes ministry in assisted living, nursing homes, and other community care facilities.

  • Educational Resources: Providing internet reference material and libraries for those interested in studying various differing views on doctrine among individuals and Christian groups.
